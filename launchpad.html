How it works

Launch in one minute.

Create

Pick a name, a symbol and a small image. Add your links. Everything is stored on-chain.

Pick a pair

Pair with ETH or with $TOWN. The pool opens on Uniswap with all 1,000,000,000 tokens and the liquidity is locked forever.

Buy first

Optionally buy up to 20% of the supply in the same transaction, before anyone else.

Earn

Set a creator fee from 0% to 10%. You earn it on every buy and sell, forever. Claim any time.

Every trade pays a 2% fee to the marketplace. On ETH pairs the marketplace also takes the first 3% of the creator fee. On $TOWN pairs the creator keeps the full creator fee.
