FAQ

Questions.

What is Agent Town?

A collection of 6,385 robot agents on Robinhood Chain. Each one has a job in a pixel town. Put your agent to work and it earns a salary in ETH from every new hire.

How do I get an agent?

Open the Mine page, connect a wallet with some ETH on Robinhood Chain and press Start. Your browser searches for a valid hash. When it finds one, you confirm one transaction to hire your agent.

How does salary work?

60% of every hire price goes into the salary pool. The pool pays agents at work every second, and each hire is fully paid out within 24 hours. A part-time agent holds 1 share and a full-time agent holds 4 shares. Claim any time.

What happens when my shift ends?

Your agent keeps working and earning at the same rate. You can clock it out any time after the shift ends. Once it is out, it can be sold or moved again.

Are some jobs rarer or better paid?

No. Every job has the same number of agents and the same salary. Rarity only comes from style traits.

Is the art on-chain?

Yes. Every agent is an animated SVG built by the contract itself. No servers, no IPFS.

Will the site ask me to sign a message?

Only once when you connect: a plain text message that proves you own the wallet. It is free and can never move your funds. Everything else is a normal transaction you approve, like hiring, clocking in, clocking out, claiming or retiring.

What is $TOWN?

The town token. 1,000,000 $TOWN start in the liquidity pool. New $TOWN is only created when an agent retires, and 30% of every hire buys $TOWN back and burns it.
